# Welcome to the Quantum Blue Wiki

**Quantum Blue** is an enterprise-grade cryptographic ecosystem designed to secure digital assets, financial transactions, and software supply chains against the existential threat of quantum computing. 

By leveraging **NIST-approved Lattice-Based Cryptography (ML-DSA)**, Quantum Blue provides organizations with mathematically unbreakable signing capabilities, deployed via a high-performance Go-based daemon and a globally distributed edge network.
